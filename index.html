FAQ

Before we build together.

What exactly do you do?+

We identify monetizable problems inside a creator's existing content and audience, then turn demonstrated expertise into a creator-native digital product system.

Do I need an existing digital product?+

No. The operating process is designed to identify and architect an opportunity from your content, expertise and audience signals.

How large does my audience need to be?+

The current positioning is aimed at creators with roughly 10K–100K followers and an engaged audience.

Who owns the product?+

Ownership and partnership terms should be discussed during the application; the landing page does not prescribe a fixed ownership model.

How does the partnership / revenue model work?+

The model is positioned as a partnership rather than a standard subscription. Exact revenue-share percentages are discussed during the application.

How long does product development take?+

Timing depends on the product opportunity, scope and creator participation. It is intentionally not fixed on this page.

What platforms can the product be sold on?+

The specific platform can be selected around the product, audience and delivery requirements during the partnership discussion.
